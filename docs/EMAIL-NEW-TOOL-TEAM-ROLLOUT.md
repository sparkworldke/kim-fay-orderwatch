# Kim-Fay Sight Rollout Emails

This document contains separate, send-ready emails for each rollout audience. Send the general announcement first, followed by the relevant team email with its training and access details.

---

## 1. General Announcement

### Subject

**Introducing Kim-Fay Sight: Our New Operations and Customer Visibility Tool**

### Email

Dear Team,

We are introducing **Kim-Fay Sight**, a new operational tool that brings together production visibility, customer and sales activity, order tracking, and KP workflows.

The objective is to give teams a consistent source of operational information, reduce manual follow-ups, and make it easier to identify issues requiring action.

The system will be introduced in phases:

1. Production workflows and operational visibility.
2. Customer Care, Sales order visibility, and customer portfolios.
3. KP Free on Loan workflows.
4. KP CRM and expanded customer-management workflows at a later stage.

Access will be based on each user's role, department, reporting line, and assigned customer portfolio. The menus and information visible to you may therefore differ from those available to another team member.

Each team will receive a separate communication covering its features, responsibilities, training, and rollout date.

Regards,  
**Product Management**  
Kim-Fay East Africa

## 2. Production Team Email

### Subject

**Kim-Fay Sight for Production: Planning, Output and Performance Visibility**

### Email

Dear Production Team,

As part of the phased introduction of **Kim-Fay Sight**, the Production team will receive access to tools designed to improve production planning and performance visibility.

### What You Will Be Able to View

- Production requirements and priorities.
- Monthly production output.
- Machine assignments.
- Production performance summaries.
- Product and inventory information relevant to planning.
- Sales and order signals used to support production decisions.
- Fulfilment and stock information requiring Production action.

The objective is to help the team identify demand, monitor output, prioritize production requirements, and work from the same operational data used by the commercial and operations teams.

1. You can sign in and access the Production module.
2. The products shown have the correct descriptions and classifications.

6. Performance summaries reconcile with the supporting production data.

Please report discrepancies using the applicable inventory ID, machine, production period, or order number. This will allow the support team to trace the source record quickly.

Training and the Production rollout date will be communicated separately.

Regards,

## 3. Customer Care and Sales Team Email

### Subject

**Kim-Fay Sight for Customer Care and Sales: Orders, Customers and Sales Intelligence**

### Email

Dear Customer Care and Sales Team,

As part of the phased introduction of **Kim-Fay Sight**, Customer Care and Sales users will receive access to order, customer, fulfilment, and portfolio information relevant to their responsibilities.

### Available Features

- Sales orders and current order status.
- Pending approval, shipping, completed, rejected, open, and on-hold orders.
- Backorders, fill rate, and items not delivered.
- Customer, status, customer segment, brand, and consultant filters.
- Customer portfolio and performance information.
- Sales Intelligence views for the applicable business channel.

### Customer Portfolio Access

Sales Consultants will see their own name in the Consultant filter and will only see customers assigned to their portfolio.

HODs and eligible managers will be able to view their reportees, review combined team performance, drill down to an individual reportee, and view the customers assigned to that reportee.

Customer assignments use the **Acumatica customer ID** as the common reference between Acumatica and Kim-Fay Sight. Acumatica employee numbers and representative codes help reconcile consultant identities, but approved customer mappings remain the source of truth for portfolio visibility.

### What We Need You to Verify

When access is provided, please confirm that:

1. Your name, role, department, manager, employee number, and representative code are correct.
2. You can see the correct menus for your responsibilities.
3. The customers and outlets shown belong to your portfolio.
4. Customers are grouped under the correct parent account where applicable.
5. Orders and statuses agree with the corresponding Acumatica records.
6. Dashboard totals, graphs, and lists remain consistent when the same filters are applied.
7. HOD reporting lines and reportee portfolios are correct.

Please report any incorrect or missing assignment with the customer name, Acumatica customer ID, consultant name, and employee or representative code. Do not share screenshots or exported customer information outside approved business teams.

Training and the Customer Care and Sales rollout date will be communicated separately.

## 4. KP Team Email: FOL Release

### Subject

**Kim-Fay Sight for KP: Free on Loan Workflow Launch**

### Email

Dear KP Team,

We are introducing the first KP workflow in **Kim-Fay Sight: Free on Loan (FOL)**.

This release provides a consistent process for requesting, reviewing, approving, and tracking Free on Loan equipment issued to customers.

### What the FOL Module Supports

- Creating a Free on Loan request for a customer.
- Selecting approved dispensers or other FOL equipment.
- Selecting consumables used to support the request.
- Reviewing the customer's previous three- and six-month consumable sales.
- Attaching the required supporting documents.
- Reviewing and approving requests through the defined workflow.
- Maintaining a clear record of equipment issued to customers.

### Equipment and Consumables

Inventory items are classified as:

- **FOL Item:** Equipment or a dispenser that may be issued to a customer on a Free on Loan basis.
- **Consumable:** A product used to provide supporting customer sales evidence.
- **Both:** An item permitted in either classification where this has been approved.

The equipment being issued and the consumables used as sales evidence must be selected separately. The same item must not be used as both the issued equipment and supporting consumable within the same request.

### Information Required for a Request

Before submitting a live FOL request, confirm:

1. The correct customer and Acumatica customer ID.
2. The requested FOL equipment and quantity.
3. The supporting consumables.
4. The available three- and six-month customer sales information.
5. The business justification.
6. The required attachments and customer documents.
7. The correct requestor and approval route.

### KP CRM Will Follow Later

This release focuses on the **FOL workflow only**.

KP CRM, including customer accounts, meetings, follow-ups, dormant-account activity, and expanded KP sales visibility, will be introduced in a later phase after the FOL workflow has stabilized and the initial customer data has been validated.

KP CRM access will be restricted to approved KP, Administration, Executive, and C-suite users. Receiving access to FOL does not automatically provide access to future KP CRM information.

### What We Need You to Verify

When access is provided, please confirm that:

1. You can access KP Operations and the FOL module.
2. The available dispensers and equipment are correctly classified as FOL Items.
3. The supporting products are correctly classified as Consumables.
4. Customer searches return the correct Acumatica customer.
5. Three- and six-month sales evidence is associated with the correct customer and consumables.
6. The approval workflow sends the request to the correct reviewers.
7. Submitted requests retain their attachments, item selections, evidence, and approval history.

Please report discrepancies using the customer ID, inventory ID, request number, and a short description of the issue.

Training, user access, and the FOL go-live date will be communicated separately. Please do not submit a live customer request until the go-live confirmation has been issued.

Regards,  
**Product Management**  
Kim-Fay East Africa
